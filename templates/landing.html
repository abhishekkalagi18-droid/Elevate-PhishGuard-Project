Operational Workflow

1

Ethics & Privacy

Built with Strict Ethical Guardrails

Security awareness training must never compromise user privacy, breach trust, or collect genuine credentials.

Zero Password Harvesting

PhishGuard forms never intercept or store actual passwords, authentication tokens, or session cookies. Simulated links immediately intercept and educate.

Strict Allowlist Enforcement

Simulations are restricted to verified domain allowlists. The platform rejects any dispatch to unverified external mail servers or third-party targets.

Non-Punitive Education

Phishing drills are not designed to shame or punish employees. Educational micro-lessons turn mistakes into low-friction learning opportunities.

Offline & Local First

Run drills safely in an isolated sandbox with SQLite and local Mailpit SMTP, keeping all test telemetry strictly within your internal boundary.
