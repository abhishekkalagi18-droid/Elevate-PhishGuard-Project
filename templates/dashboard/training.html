Constructive Learning Modules

Targeted micro-lessons activated immediately upon simulation interactions

Preview Teachable Moment
Module 01 3 min read

Sender Verification & Lookalike Domains

How to spot typo-squatted domains, subdomain spoofing (e.g., it-support.co vs company.internal), and display name impersonation.

Key Takeaway: Display names can be set to anything. Always inspect the raw sending domain after the '@' sign.
Module 02 4 min read

Deconstructing Artificial Urgency

Psychological levers used by social engineers: threat of account suspension, short deadlines (e.g. 4 hours), and emotional coercion.

Key Takeaway: Real organizational emergencies follow documented escalation paths, never urgent unverified email links.
Module 03 3 min read

Hyperlink Hygiene & Hover Inspection

Inspecting destination URLs before clicking. Understanding shortened links, redirect chains, and deceptive anchor text.

Key Takeaway: Hover before you click. If the anchor text says "Internal Portal" but points elsewhere, report it immediately.
Module 04 5 min read

Business Email Compromise (BEC) & Invoices

Targeted attacks against Finance and Operations impersonating executives or known vendors demanding urgent wire modifications.

Key Takeaway: Always perform out-of-band verification (phone call to known number) before altering wire instructions.

The PhishGuard Psychological Safety Framework

Why positive reinforcement produces superior long-term retention compared to punitive measures:

1. Immediate Feedback

Interventions delivered within seconds of a click produce 4x higher retention than quarterly scheduled webinars.

2. Blame-Free Culture

Punitive environments cause employees to hide mistakes. Psychological safety encourages prompt threat reporting.

3. Gamified Mastery

Micro-quizzes and verifiable resilience badges give participants tangible milestones in their professional development.
