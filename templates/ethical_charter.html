Ethical Guidelines & Governance

The PhishGuard Ethical Simulation Charter

Phishing simulations are a defensive tool designed exclusively to build human resilience. PhishGuard enforces strict non-punitive, privacy-centric standards.

1. Absolute Prohibition of Real Credential Collection

PhishGuard landing pages never request, collect, store, or log actual employee passwords, multi-factor codes, API keys, or session tokens. Any user who clicks a simulated link is immediately routed to a safe teachable moment screen that clearly indicates the email was part of an internal drill.

2. Domain Allowlisting & Target Consent

Drills are strictly restricted to corporate-owned domains configured in the authorized allowlist. Dispatches to unverified external domains are blocked at the engine level to prevent accidental harassment or spamming of third parties.

3. Non-Punitive, Educational Philosophy

Employees who click simulated links must never be penalized, docked compensation, or subjected to public embarrassment. Simulations serve to uncover systemic vulnerabilities and train pattern-recognition in a constructive, supportive environment.

4. Data Minimization & Privacy Protection

Interaction telemetry is limited to operational timestamps (opened, clicked, reported, trained) and synthetic risk calculations. Personal device files, external web history, or unauthorized trackers are never queried or recorded.
